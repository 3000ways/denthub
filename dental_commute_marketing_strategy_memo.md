## The one-line strategy

We are the **honest, curated ranking of dental media** — Wirecutter for dentists. We don't need to buy attention; we need to *earn* it by being the resource that dentists, students, and podcast hosts genuinely want to share. Every free play below works because a curated directory is inherently *useful to give away* — it's not an ad, it's a recommendation.

**Our unfair angle:** transparent scoring + flagging AI-narrated podcasts. Nobody else in dental media is doing "honest broker." That's the hook that turns a link-drop into a story.

## Messaging angles (lean on the differentiator)

Reuse these across every channel — adapt the wording, keep the substance:

1. **"An honest, ranked guide to dental media."** Not another list — a *scored* ranking with a transparent formula. Position against ad-stuffed listicles.
2. **"We flag AI-narrated podcasts."** This is genuinely novel and *news-pitchable*. It's the headline that gets DrBicuspid / Dentistry Today / a HARO journalist to bite where a plain "new directory" wouldn't.
3. **"Free, and built for students."** For ASDA/FCDSA/LibGuides/Reddit — a free curated resource kit by year of school. No paywall, no catch.
4. **"Claim your show."** For podcast hosts — we already rank you; come see your score rationale and cross-promote. Flatters the creator and recruits them.

## The three content pieces that earn links on autopilot

Publishing these on our own site turns us from a *pitcher* of links into a *magnet* for them. Prioritize #1 and #2.

1. **"The Best Dental Podcasts of 2026 — Ranked."** Annual, transparently scored. Annual "best of" lists are the single most-cited content format — every roundup writer links to the ranker. Refresh yearly for compounding links.
2. **"Which Dental Podcasts Are AI-Narrated? An Honest Audit."** A data report powered by our AI-voice disclosure system. Novel, quotable, and the exact thing journalists and Qwoted/Source-of-Sources queries link to. This is your PR flagship.
3. **"The Dental Student's Free Resource Kit."** Curated by year of school — built to be the canonical thing ASDA chapters, librarians, and r/predental wikis link to.

## ⚠️ Do NOT do this (how to not get banned or waste effort)

- **Do not link-drop in Reddit or Facebook groups.** r/Dentistry, r/askdentists, Dental Nachos, Nifty Thrifty, and Dentaltown all effectively **ban unapproved self-promo** — low-karma link posts auto-remove and blatant promo gets you banned. The *only* safe pattern: contribute genuine value first, and share the site **only** when someone directly asks "what's a good podcast/resource?" For anything more, modmail/admin approval first.
- **Don't treat ASDA / Group Dentistry Now / DEO as organic-posting grounds.** They're editorial/partnership (or paid) channels — win them with a pitch or a guest article, not a wall post.
- **Skip r/askdentists and the mega patient-facing YouTubers (Dental Digest 20M, The Bentist 3.3M) for now.** Huge reach but wrong audience (patients, not the dentists/students we serve) and heavily gatekept. Low ROI relative to effort.
- **Vet the low-DA "write for us" blogs** (Tooth Remedy, DentalsReview, etc.) before pitching — some skirt link-farm territory and a bad neighborhood link can hurt more than help. Opportunistic only.
- **Don't over-invest in podcast directories** (Podchaser, Listen Notes) — they index individual *shows*, not directory sites, so the link value to us is limited until we run our own audio.

## Data-quality caveats (verify before outreach)

The research was web-sourced and mostly verified, but a few items need a 30-second confirm before you act:

- **Reddit subscriber counts and several Facebook group sizes** are marked UNVERIFIED — Reddit and those pages blocked automated fetching. Confirm live numbers and each group's current pinned rules.
- **Some YouTube handles/sub counts** (Dr. Teeth, Hack Dentistry, Mental Dental) are approximate — confirm the exact channel URL before outreach.
- **Dentistry Today / Weave / FeedSpot pages** returned bot-blocks (403) — the outlets are real and confirmed via search, but double-check the live submission contact.

Every row in the CSV carries a `rules_or_gotchas` and `notes` column flagging these.
